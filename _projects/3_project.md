## Overview

My journey into robotics began with Formula Student during my undergraduate studies at Harbin Institute of Technology. As Team Leader of the [HIT Racing Team](http://www.hrtcn.org/) in Season 2015-2016, I worked with more than 60 students to design, manufacture, integrate, and test a fully electric Formula Student race car.

Unlike a typical coursework project, Formula Student was a complete engineering experience. We were responsible for every aspect of the vehicle, from mechanical design and electrical architecture to embedded control systems, manufacturing, testing, project management, sponsorship, and competition.

The project taught me how to build reliable engineering systems under tight deadlines and collaborate within a large multidisciplinary team. Many of the skills I use today in robotics system integration, rapid prototyping, and experimental validation were first developed during this experience.

## Highlights

- Team Leader and day-to-day management of the HIT Racing EV Team (60+ members) in Season 2015-2016
- Racing car driver
- founder of HIT Autonomous Racing Team in 2016.
- Designed and integrated electrical and powertrain systems
- Led vehicle testing and competition preparation
- Best Electrical System Design Award, Student Formula Japan 2016
- Formula Student Electric China 2016 Champion
- Over CNY 400,000 in competition prize funding in 2016
