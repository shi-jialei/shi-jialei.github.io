Our project, **“Everting Robot Endoscopy for Next Generation Theranostics,”** has received **£70,000 from Imperial College London’s [UKRI Impact Acceleration Account (IAA)](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/about/leadership-and-strategy/provost/vice-provost-research/research-strategy/funding-opportunities/internal-funding-opportunities/ukri-iaa/supported-projects/)** under the **Innovation for a Healthy Society** theme.

The project is led by **Professor Ferdinando Rodriguez y Baena**, with **James Kinross and me as co-applicants**. It builds directly on my postdoctoral research into a self-propelled soft everting robotic colonoscope. I led and wrote the proposal in collaboration with Professor Ferdinando Rodriguez y Baena and James Kinross, developing my postdoctoral work towards a translational project.

The funding period runs from **February 2026 to February 2027**. The project aims to advance the TRL of the device, supporting its further development and future clinical translation.
