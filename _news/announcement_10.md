The IEEE Transactions on Robotics King-Sun Fu Memorial Best Paper Award is one of the most prestigious publication awards in robotics. It is presented annually by the IEEE Robotics and Automation Society to recognize the best paper published in the journal IEEE Transactions on Robotics during the preceding year.

Details of our soft-everting robot are reported in the [IEEE T-RO paper](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/11112617), with video avaliable in [IEEE T-RO Youtube Channel](https://www.youtube.com/@ieeetro).
